## Featured Case Study: IAM Offboarding Control Testing

**Portfolio [Document #8 — IAM Control Testing Simulation v1.0](<https://github.com/jamaldavison/Northstar-HealthTech-IAM-Governance/blob/main/Northstar_HealthTech_IAM_Control_Testing_Simulation_v1.0_Portfolio_Reviewed.docx>)**

This independent educational exercise demonstrates how I applied a structured IAM control-testing methodology to a fictional employee-offboarding scenario.

**Control objective:** Verify that departing employees' accounts are disabled no later than their authorized departure times.

### Initial Simulated Finding

A constructed employee record showed a Microsoft Entra ID account disabled **45 minutes after the authorized departure time**. I documented this fictional timing exception as **SIM-FIND-001** and linked it to the project's **IAM-001** risk scenario.

### Risk Assessment and Corrective Action

The exercise used an illustrative **High (16/25)** inherent risk rating from the fictional risk methodology. I developed **SIM-CAP-001**, a proposed action plan addressing cause investigation, access-removal verification, deadline alerts, and retesting.

### Fictional Retest Analysis

| Simulated timing outcome | Count |
|---|---:|
| Passed | 4 |
| Failed | 1 |
| Pass rate | 80% |

One fictional account was disabled **six minutes late**. Because all five examples were required to meet the deadline, the **simulated timing criterion failed**. This percentage is not a measure of real organizational compliance.

### Governance Recommendation

**Keep SIM-CAP-001 open.** Investigate the recurring timing weakness and require implementation evidence, satisfactory testing, and independent verification before any closure decision.

**Case-study skills:** Evidence-to-control mapping, control-testing analysis, illustrative risk assessment, corrective-action planning, test criteria, and management reporting.

All case records and outcomes are fictional. The analysis is not a completed operational retest, audit, or remediation effort.

## Project Methodology

I developed these deliverables as one connected fictional IAM governance program rather than eight unrelated templates.

1. **Establish responsibilities.** Develop an IAM Governance Charter covering proposed oversight and accountability.
2. **Define IAM requirements.** Document joiner, mover, and leaver controls; access approvals; privileged access safeguards; and access reviews.
3. **Assess risks.** Build an illustrative risk register linked to controls and proposed treatment owners.
4. **Design monitoring.** Develop an Excel dashboard with KPI definitions, calculation logic, and tracking; check formula scenarios using fictional inputs.
5. **Design control tests.** Document seven procedures for provisioning, offboarding, role changes, privileged access, MFA, access reviews, and emergency privileges.
6. **Demonstrate analysis.** Evaluate constructed offboarding timestamps, document a fictional finding, plan remediation, and analyze a failed simulated timing retest.

### Evidence and Validation Boundaries

- **Designed:** Governance procedures and control-testing approaches.
- **Functionally checked:** Dashboard formulas with fictional sample inputs.
- **Simulated:** IAM evidence, risk scenarios, findings, and timestamp calculations.
- **Not Tested:** Production operation of the seven IAM controls.

No simulated activity is represented as real client work, employment experience, or verified production performance.
