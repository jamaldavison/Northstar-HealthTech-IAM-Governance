**Portfolio case study | Jamal Davison**

## 1. Scenario
A fictional 250-person health-technology company needs defensible identity lifecycle governance and offboarding controls.

## 2. My role and contribution
Created an eight-deliverable IAM governance portfolio and analyzed constructed offboarding evidence as an educational simulation.

## 4. Decisions and reasoning
In the simulated offboarding test, one account was disabled 45 minutes late; the simulated retest passed four of five samples, so the timing criterion still failed. Recommended keeping the fictional corrective-action plan open.

## 5. Results and verification
The linked deliverables are available for review in this repository. All identities, findings, timestamps, and risk ratings are fictional; no production identity system was tested.

## 6. What I would do next
With real organizational authorization, validate scope and assumptions with stakeholders, collect actual evidence, run appropriate tests, document exceptions, and seek accountable approval before any production rollout or closure claim.

## 7. Skills demonstrated
Security governance, risk assessment, evidence traceability, clear documentation, stakeholder-ready reporting, and separating proposed controls from verified results.
